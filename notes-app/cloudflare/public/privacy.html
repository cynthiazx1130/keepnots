守心便签（Keep Notes）隐私政策

生效日期：2026-10-12 · English below
一句话总结：我们不收集你的任何数据。便签只存在你自己的设备和你自己配置的网盘里；没有账户、没有广告、没有统计追踪。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

我们不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人信息到开发者的服务器。应用没有账户系统，不嵌入任何统计 SDK、广告 SDK 或崩溃上报服务。

2. 你的便签存在哪里

  • 本机存储：便签以标准 Markdown（.md）文件保存在你在设备上选择的位置，并在应用数据库中保留一份缓存副本。
  • 你自己的网盘：如果你配置了坚果云或其他 WebDAV 网盘同步，便签会加密传输并保存到你自己名下的网盘账户。该网盘的登录凭据只保存在你的设备上，仅用于连接你指定的网盘，不会发送给开发者或任何第三方。

3. 加密

加密便签采用 AES-256-GCM 对称加密与 PBKDF2（150,000 次迭代）派生密钥。加密密码只存在于你的设备上，永不离开你的设备；开发者无法读取、也无法帮你恢复忘记密码的加密内容。

4. 权限用途

  • 网络访问：仅用于向你自己配置的网盘（WebDAV）同步便签。
  • 文件存储：仅用于把便签以 .md 文件写入你在设备上选择的文件夹。

5. 第三方

本应用不与任何第三方共享、出售或分析你的数据。你选择使用的网盘服务商（如坚果云）与 Google Play 作为独立的平台服务，各自受其自身隐私政策约束。

6. 数据删除

删除便签、卸载应用或清除应用数据，即删除设备上的便签数据；网盘中的数据可在你的网盘账户中自行删除。开发者没有任何可以删除的服务器数据——因为我们本来就不存。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向儿童收集任何数据，也不含任何年龄受限内容。

8. 联系方式

如对本政策有疑问，请通过 Google Play 商店页面提供的开发者联系方式与我们联系。

Keep Notes Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-12 · 回到中文版顶部
In one line: we collect nothing. Your notes live only on your device and in the cloud drive you configure. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

1. What we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to the developer. The app has no account system and embeds no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

2. Where your notes live

  • On your device: notes are saved as standard Markdown (.md) files in a folder you choose, with a cache copy in the app database.
  • Your own cloud drive: if you enable sync, notes are stored in your own Jianguoyun or other WebDAV account. Those credentials stay on your device and are used only to connect to the drive you chose — never sent to the developer or any third party.

3. Encryption

Encrypted notes use AES-256-GCM with keys derived via PBKDF2 (150,000 iterations). The password never leaves your device. The developer cannot read encrypted notes or recover a forgotten password.

4. Permissions

  • Network access: used only to sync notes to the WebDAV drive you configure.
  • File storage: used only to write .md note files into the folder you pick on your device.

5. Third parties

We never share, sell, or analyze your data with anyone. Your chosen drive provider (e.g. Jianguoyun) and Google Play act as independent platforms under their own privacy policies.

6. Deleting your data

Deleting notes, uninstalling the app, or clearing its data removes the on-device copies; cloud copies can be deleted in your own drive account. There is no developer server holding your data — there never was.

7. Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children, and contains no age-restricted content.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, reach us through the developer contact listed on the Google Play store page.
